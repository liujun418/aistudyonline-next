# Codex Agent: Your Desktop Jarvis for Effortless Task Automation

In the ever\-evolving landscape of AI tools, OpenAI’s Codex has dropped a game\-changing update that’s turning heads in the tech community\. ## 1\. Getting Started: Activate Codex’s Desktop Control

First, you need to unlock Codex’s ability to interact with your desktop apps\. Here’s how:

- **Access the Sidebar \&amp; Settings**: Navigate to the sidebar in your Codex interface, click on “Settings”, and find the “Computer Use” section\.

- **Install the Plugin**: Locate the dedicated plugin for desktop integration and install it\. This plugin is the bridge that lets Codex “see” and control your apps\.

## 2\. Command Codex with Natural Language \(and @Mentions\)

The beauty of Codex’s new agent lies in its simplicity\. You don’t need to be a coding wizard—just use natural language, and tag apps with `@`\.

### Example 1: Build a Personal Website with Claude

Want a personal homepage? Tell Codex:

```Plain Text
@Claude 帮我创建一个个人主页，要求设计现代简约，包含作品集和联系表单。
```

Hit enter, and Codex will open Claude, generate the website code, and even launch it in your browser\. The result? A polished, professional page that’s ready to impress—no web development skills required\.

### Example 2: Generate Visuals with ChatGPT

Need custom graphics for your overseas site? Command Codex like this:

```Plain Text
@ChatGPT 生成一组关于“跨境电商趋势”的漫画风格图片，要求色彩鲜明，风格统一。
```

Codex will trigger ChatGPT’s image\-generation capabilities \(powered by Image 1\.5, since 2\.0 is still in beta\) and deliver visuals you can use for blogs, social media, or product pages\.

## 3\. Automate Complex Workflows Across Apps

The real magic happens when you chain tasks across multiple applications\. Let’s walk through a practical workflow for an overseas e\-commerce site owner:

### Step 1: Design Inspiration \&amp; Replication

Find a design you love for your site:

```Plain Text
@Chrome 在Dribbble上找一个高端电商网站的设计风格，然后让@Claude 复刻这个风格到我的 landing page。
```

Codex will open Chrome, search Dribbble, pick a design, and instruct Claude to replicate it—automatically opening the preview in your browser\.

### Step 2: Set the Mood with Music

Keep your creative flow going while working on your site:

```Plain Text
@网易云音乐 播放《New Boy》，然后记录我完成这一步的心情和难度，用内置图像生成功能做两张图，保存到本地。
```

Codex will launch NetEase Cloud Music, play the song, and then use its image\-generation tool to create visuals that capture your workflow’s “vibe”—perfect for sharing behind\-the\-scenes content on your site’s blog or socials\.

### Step 3: Organize and Access Files

Finally, manage the assets Codex creates:

```Plain Text
@访达 定位到刚才生成的图片文件，给@Claude 完全访问权限，以便后续编辑。
```

Codex will open Finder \(or your OS’s file manager\), locate the files, and set permissions—all without you lifting a finger\.
