# Full Practical Guide to AI Comic Drama Production: From Zero to Published Video

## Introduction

AI comic drama is a highly profitable form of animated short drama that allows audiences to unlock episodes by either paying or watching ads\. In this guide, we will walk you through the complete process of creating an AI comic drama, from initial scene description to final post\-production, using free AI tools\.

## Core Production Workflow

The AI video production process consists of four key steps:

1. Scene Description

2. Text\-to\-Image Generation

3. Image\-to\-Video Generation

4. Post\-Production Editing

## Step 1: Scene Description

The first step is to clearly describe the scene you want to create\. We recommend using an AI assistant to help refine your scene description\.

### Example Prompt for Scene Description

```Plain Text
I will provide you with a scene, please help me refine it into a detailed scene description, do not generate images: a girl standing by a grassland, with long white hair, red eyes, wearing a large white hat and a white dress, looking at the camera.
```

### Key Tips for Scene Description

- Be clear about what you will provide to the AI

- Specify what you want the AI to do

- Clearly state what you don't want the AI to do

## Step 2: Text\-to\-Image Generation

Once you have a refined scene description, you can generate images based on it\.

### Example Prompt for Text\-to\-Image Generation

```Plain Text
Generate an image for this scene, with a 16:9 aspect ratio, using a flat Japanese anime style.
```

### Key Tips for Text\-to\-Image Generation

- Always specify the aspect ratio \(16:9 is recommended for horizontal videos\)

- Specify the art style \(e\.g\., flat Japanese anime style\)

- Generate multiple images and select the best one

## Step 3: Image\-to\-Video Generation

After selecting the best image, you can generate a video from it\.

### Example Prompt for Image\-to\-Video Generation

```Plain Text
Generate a video from this image, with a 16:9 aspect ratio. The girl says: "I have forgotten my original name, just call me Bai. Do you remember your name?" Her voice is soft, and the wind gently blows her hair.
```

### Key Tips for Image\-to\-Video Generation

- Use tools like Xiaoyunque for cost\-effective video generation

- Specify the duration of the video \(10 seconds is recommended for beginners\)

- Let the AI freely add details to the video

## Step 4: Post\-Production Editing

The final step is to edit the video using a video editing software like Jianying\.

### Key Post\-Production Tasks

1. **Add Sound Effects**: Add sound effects that match the scene \(e\.g\., wind blowing through grass\)

2. **Adjust Audio Levels**: Adjust the volume of the sound effects to match the video

3. **Add Subtitles**: Add subtitles to the video

4. **Trim the Video**: Trim the video to the desired length

### Example Subtitle Content

```Plain Text
I have forgotten my original name, just call me Bai.
Do you remember your name?
```

## Script Creation with AI

The core of AI script creation is precise prompting, which involves three key points:

1. **Who**: Assign a role to the AI

2. **What**: Clearly define the task for the AI

3. **How**: Specify the output format and style

### Example Prompt for Script Creation

```Plain Text
You are a skilled animation screenwriter specializing in one-minute healing animation series. Please write a one-minute animation core plot around the theme of "companionship", with the protagonist being a cartoon cat.
```

## Conclusion

By following these four steps, you can create a high\-quality AI comic drama from scratch\. Remember to refine your prompts, experiment with different tools, and iterate on your work to achieve the best results\.
