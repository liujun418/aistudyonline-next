# 2025 AI Toolkit: A Practical Guide for Every Domain

In 2025, the AI landscape has evolved dramatically, with thousands of new tools hitting the market\. Navigating this vast ecosystem can be overwhelming, but this guide breaks down the best AI tools across key domains, helping you save time and money while maximizing productivity\.

## 1\. Text AI Tools

When it comes to text\-based AI, choosing the right tool is crucial\.

- **Top Pick: Gemini 3\.0 Pro**
Priced at ¥140/month, it stands out with its **long context window** and **multimodal capabilities**\. It tops benchmarks for most task types, and the latest Gemini Flash is free, making it a no\-brainer for comprehensive text tasks\.

- **Free Alternative: Doubao**
It’s incredibly well\-rounded, covering a wide range of text\-related needs without any cost\.

- **Open\-Source Option: Qwen**
Unlike Deepseek, which is often too large and difficult to deploy, Qwen offers multiple model sizes \(0\.5B, 1\.5B, 14B, 72B\), catering to different performance and speed requirements\.

- **Others**:

    - ChatGPT: Versatile but requires a subscription \(Personal Plus: ¥140/month, Advanced: ¥200/month\)\.

    - Grok: Great for Twitter scraping and real\-time updates\.

    - Claude: Strong for long texts and document analysis\.

    - Kimi, DeepSeek: Free to use, each with unique strengths\.

*Actionable Tip*: Use Grok as a Twitter scraper with prompts like:

```plaintext
Scrape the latest tweets from [username] about AI trends.
```

## 2\. Image Generation Tools

AI image generation has seen remarkable advancements\.

- **Top Pick: Nano Banana Pro**
At ¥1 per 2K image via API, it’s easy to use, offers **consistent results**, and allows for convenient editing with high\-quality outputs\.

- **Alternative: Seedream\-4\.5**
With an API price of ¥0\.25 per 2K image, it excels in consistency, multi\-reference support, and editing capabilities, offering great value for money\.

- **Open\-Source: Z\-image**
A new favorite, it works with just 6GB of VRAM, making it accessible even for low\-end systems\. It delivers excellent Chinese text understanding and high\-quality visuals\.

- **Others**:

    - Midjourney: Artistic and diverse, with subscriptions starting at ¥70/month\.

    - Flux: Free and open\-source, suitable for specific artistic styles\.

## 3\. Video AI Tools

Video AI tools have become more sophisticated\.

- **Top Pick: Veo 3\.1**
It offers **perfect audio\-visual synchronization**, natural motion, and high\-quality outputs, costing around ¥14 for 5 seconds\. It’s ideal for producing polished videos\.

- **Alternative: Seedance\-1\.5\-Pro**
Priced at ¥4 for 5 seconds, it provides synchronized audio\-visuals, detailed performances, and high cost\-effectiveness\.

- **Open\-Source: Wan 2\.2**
It offers rich features and video quality comparable to closed\-source tools, making it a solid free option\.

- **Others**:

    - KeLing, Vidu: Great for character\-based video production with reference images\.

    - Conch: Excels in motion capture\.

    - Runway: Offers excellent editing effects\.

## 4\. Audio AI Tools

### Voiceover

- **Top Pick: MiniMax**
With a subscription of 36mb/month, it’s easy to use, cost\-effective, and can generate natural\-sounding voices in various tones\.

- **Advanced: ElevenLabs**
At 355mb/month \(free tier available\), it’s superior for foreign language voiceovers\.

- **Open\-Source: IndexTTS2**
Free to use, it’s great for voice cloning and text\-to\-speech, with a user\-friendly interface\.

*Actionable Tip*: Use IndexTTS2 with the following command structure for voice cloning:

```plaintext
python index_tts.py --input_audio "your_voice_sample.wav" --text "Your desired text here."
```

### Music

- **Top Pick: Suno**
At ¥70/month \(free tier allows 10 songs/day\), it generates stunning music that will amaze you\.

- **Others**:

    - Udio: Allows segmental and 精细化 adjustment of music\.

    - Stable Audio: Excels at precise beat matching\.

## 5\. Digital Human Tools

- **Top Pick: HeyGen**
With a subscription of 203mb/month, it delivers high\-quality digital human videos with natural motions and expressions\.

- **Alternative: InfiniteTalk**
Free and open\-source, though it requires more setup effort\.

- **Replacement for Short Clips: Image\-to\-Video \(Veo3\)**
For short AI character clips with brief dialogues, use image\-to\-video tools like Veo3\.

## 6\. AI Browsers

- **Top Picks: Dia, Atlas**
Dia offers a comfortable UI, free access to GPT features, customization options, and Agent capabilities\. Atlas is also a strong choice, especially if you’re already invested in ChatGPT\.

- **Alternatives: Doubao, Kuaike**
These free domestic AI browsers are solid options for basic AI\-assisted browsing\.

## 7\. Coding AI Tools

- **Top Pick: Cursor**
With Pro at ¥140/month, Pro\+ at ¥420/month, and Ultra at ¥1400/month, it’s stable and updates rapidly, integrating tools like Claude Code and Codex seamlessly\.

- **Alternative: Trae**
At ¥21 for the first month and ¥70/month thereafter, it has excellent product details and is user\-friendly even for beginners\.

- **Advanced: Claude Code, Codex \(GPT5 Series\)**
Claude Code \(Pro: ¥100/month, Max 5x: ¥700/month, Max 20x: ¥1400/month\) and Codex \(Plus: ¥140/month, Pro: ¥210/month\) are powerful for complex coding tasks, with Codex often fixing bugs from Claude\.

- **Entry\-Level: Google AI Studio \(Build Mode\)**
Free to use, it’s great for creating creative coding demos with Gemini\.

## 8\. Knowledge Base Tools

- **Top Pick: NotebookLM**
Free to use, it’s a multi\-resource learning powerhouse\. You can dump a large amount of data into it to generate audio summaries, mind maps, presentation documents, infographics, and video summaries\.

- **Alternative: IMA**
It offers 30GB of free storage and is great for collecting and reading public accounts\.

## 9\. Agent Tools

- **Top Pick: Codex**
At ¥140/month, it can be used as a general\-purpose Agent\.

- **Specialized: Deep Research**
Perfect for in\-depth investigations, saving you a lot of time on research tasks\.

- **Alternatives: AutoGLM, Kouzi Space**
Both are free, with AutoGLM being open\-source and Kouzi Space offering a user\-friendly interface\.

- **Workflow: n8n**
Free for local deployment, it’s great for setting up automated workflows, though it has a steep learning curve\.
