# A Complete Guide to the AI Industry Chain: From Energy to Smart Robots

If you’ve ever wondered how AI works from the ground up, this video breaks down the entire AI industry chain in simple, practical terms\. From raw materials to smart robots, you’ll learn how each part connects—and how you can find opportunities in this trillion\-dollar sector\.

## What Is AI, Really?

AI isn’t just a buzzword—it’s a system that can think, reason, and interact with humans\. To understand it, let’s start with a simple example:

- **Basic AI \(e\.g\., "Potato" robot\)**: Can only match keywords\. If you ask, "Am I handsome?" it can’t respond because the system wasn’t programmed with that answer\.

- **Advanced AI \(e\.g\., "Ergou" robot\)**: Can reason, recognize intent, and even understand human emotions\. When asked the same question, it might reply, "You’re very cute\!"

The key difference? Advanced AI can process context and make decisions beyond pre\-programmed responses\.

## The AI Industry Chain: From Raw Materials to Smart Robots

### 1\. Energy: The Foundation of AI

AI systems need massive amounts of electricity to operate\. This is why energy is the most fundamental part of the AI industry chain\.

**Key Insight**: "The end of AI is electricity; the end of electricity is energy\."

- **New Energy**: Countries are competing to develop new energy sources \(solar, wind, nuclear\) to power AI\.

- **Fossil Fuels**: Traditional energy sources \(oil, coal\) still play a role in powering AI infrastructure\.

### 2\. Chips: The Brain of AI

Chips are the "brain" of AI systems\. They process data and run algorithms\.

**How Chips Are Made**:

1. **Raw Material**: Chips start as sand \(specifically quartz sand ore\)\.

2. **Purification**: The sand is purified to 11\-nines \(99\.999999999% pure\) silicon\.

3. **Ingot \& Wafer**: The silicon is melted into an ingot, then cut into thin wafers\.

4. **Lithography**: Wafers are etched with nanoscale circuits using machines like ASML’s EUV lithography systems\.

```bash
# Example: Check chip manufacturing equipment

```

### 3\. PCB \& MLCC: The Body of AI

- **PCB \(Printed Circuit Board\)**: The "skeleton" that connects chips and other components\.

- **MLCC \(Multi\-Layer Ceramic Capacitor\)**: Acts as a "micro power bank" to stabilize voltage during AI operations\.

**Why MLCC Matters**: Without stable voltage, AI systems would crash or perform poorly\.

### 4\. Large Models: The Knowledge of AI

Large models \(e\.g\., DeepSeek, Doubao\) are the "knowledge base" of AI\. They learn from massive amounts of data to become "smart\."

**How Large Models Work**:

1. **Data Collection**: Raw data is gathered from various sources\.

2. **Data Processing**: Data is cleaned and labeled by "data processing workers\."

3. **Training**: The model is trained on the processed data to learn patterns and generate responses\.


### 5\. Smart Robots: The Body of AI

Smart robots \(e\.g\., robot dogs, humanoid robots\) are the "physical body" of AI\. They allow AI to interact with the real world\.

**Current Applications**:

- **Tourism**: Robots perform dances or shows for visitors\.

- **Industry**: Robots assist with manufacturing and logistics\.

**Future Opportunities**:

- **Service Robots**: Robots for healthcare, education, and customer service\.

- **Industrial Robots**: Robots for complex tasks like assembly and maintenance\.
