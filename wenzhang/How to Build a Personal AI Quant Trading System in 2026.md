## 1\. How Large Quant Firms Profit

To succeed in quantitative trading, you must first understand how large firms operate\.

### 1\.1 The Core Strategy of Big Quant Firms

- **Example: High\-Frequency Trading \(HFT\)**

    - Firms like HF Quant use AI\-powered hedge fund strategies\.

    - They process massive data with neural networks and analyze financial/economic behavior using natural language processing \(NLP\)\.

    - Instead of predicting single stock movements, they rank thousands of stocks simultaneously\.

    - **Example**: If a model predicts Stock A has a higher probability of outperforming peers and Stock B has a lower probability, the portfolio increases A's weight and decreases B's weight\.

    - Over thousands of trades, statistical advantage ensures profitability\.

- **Probability Theory Simplified**

    - Flipping a coin 10,000 times: each side's probability approaches the theoretical value \(50%\)\.

    - Quant firms profit by identifying which side has a slightly higher probability \(e\.g\., 51% vs\. 49%\)\.

### 1\.2 The Formula for Big Quant Success

- **Strategy**: Minimal probability advantage × Extremely high trading frequency

- **Example**: 51% win rate, 100,000 trades per day

- **Why it works**: This strategy is ideal for large capital, though real\-world implementation involves risk management and hedging \(e\.g\., going long and short simultaneously\)\.

### 1\.3 Barriers to Entry for Big Quant Firms

1. **Capital volume \+ Computational power**

2. **Model speed \+ Execution system**

3. **Data access \+ Scale**

## 2\. Opportunities for Individual Traders

You can't beat big firms on speed, computing power, or data—but you can win on **information understanding** and **flexibility**\.

### 2\.1 The Flaw in Big Quant Strategies

- Large firms have too much capital, so they can't exploit small opportunities\.

- Their strategies must be stable and slow to adjust \(changing one parameter requires revalidating all parameters\)\.

### 2\.2 Your Competitive Advantage

- **Big Quant**: Industrial money\-making machine \(small advantage × high frequency\)

- **You \+ AI**: Flexible small hunter \(big advantage × low frequency\)

- **Key Insight**: Big quant firms are slow and clumsy—you can target opportunities they miss\.

### 2\.3 The Common Mistake to Avoid

- **Myth**: "Low\-frequency trading \+ certainty"

- **Reality**: There is no certainty in quantitative trading—only probability advantage\.

- **Example**: If AI predicts a 70% probability of a positive news event driving a stock up, a single trade may still fail\. The goal is to increase the win rate, not guarantee success\.

### 2\.4 Your Optimal Strategy

- **Focus**: Higher\-quality signals, lower\-frequency execution, and small\-cap opportunities\.

- **Formula**: Big advantage × low frequency \(vs\. big quant's small advantage × high frequency\)

- **Final Truth**: Success depends on your ability to see more clearly than others—not on how fast your AI is\.
