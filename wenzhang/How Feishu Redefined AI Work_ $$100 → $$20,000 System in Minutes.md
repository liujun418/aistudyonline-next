2. **AI Understanding**: The AI analyzes your requirements, clarifies ambiguities, and generates a detailed requirements document\.

3. **System Generation**: Within minutes, it creates a fully functional system with tables, dashboards, and permissions\.

## The Third Breakthrough: Multidimensional Table Agent – AI\-Powered Data Management

Manually building data tables, dragging dashboards, and configuring permissions is a thing of the past\. Feishu's Multidimensional Table Agent automates all these tasks\.

### Key Features:

- **Requirement\-Based Creation**: Describe your data needs, and the agent will generate tables, dashboards, and questionnaires automatically\.

- **Permission Automation**: It configures access rights based on your team structure\.

- **End\-to\-End Workflow**: From idea to implementation, the entire process is AI\-driven\.

### How to Use:

1. Open a multidimensional table in Feishu\.

2. Click the "Agent" button in the top\-right corner\.

3. Enter your requirements \(e\.g\., "Create a project management table with task status, assignees, and deadlines"\)\.

4. The agent will generate the table, set up a dashboard, and configure permissions\.
