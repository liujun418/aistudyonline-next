## Step 1: Automate Repetitive Tasks

The easiest way to start is by identifying tasks you repeat weekly and find tedious\. Jot them down in a notebook—these are your candidates for automation\.

### Example Automations:

1. **Growth Daily Report**:
Previously, I had to check data across multiple platforms daily\. Now, I delegate this to TRAE Work\. It automatically compiles and reports the data to me every morning\.
*Command \(to TRAE Work\):* "Automatically crawl growth data from \[platform names\] and generate a daily report\."

2. **Meeting Summary \& Action Items**:
I have many meetings during the day, but little time to organize the insights\. TRAE Work automatically reviews my Feishu meeting notes at night, extracts methodologies, creates a knowledge base, and schedules to\-dos\.
*Command \(to TRAE Work\):* "Read today’s Feishu meeting notes, extract key methodologies, create a knowledge base, and schedule action items\."

3. **Schedule Automation**:
TRAE Work can remind you of key focus areas daily or generate weekly/monthly reports\.
*Command \(to TRAE Work\):* "Remind me of key priorities every morning and generate a weekly summary\."

## Step 2: Create Custom Skills \(Skills\)

When tasks have a consistent pattern but aren’t identical, turn them into **Skills**—think of them as your personalized AI apps\.

### How to Create Skills:

1. **Find Existing Skills**:
Browse the TRAE Work community for pre\-built skills\. If none fit, ask TRAE Work to search for similar functions on platforms like GitHub or Reddit\.
*Command \(to TRAE Work\):* "I need a Skill to find viral content for my niche\. Search GitHub and Reddit for similar tools\."

2. **Customize Skills**:
Once you find a skill, modify it by simply describing your needs\.
*Example Command for Customizing a Viral Content Skill:* "I want this Skill to scrape content from \[platforms\], filter for posts with at least \[X\] likes, format the output in \[specific style\], and focus on \[specific topics\]\. Also, send the result to Feishu\."

## Step 3: Build Your AI Workbench

As you accumulate skills, you’ll want a centralized hub—the AI workbench\. This is where all your automations and skills converge\.

### Building the Workbench:

Tell TRAE Work your requirements for the web page, including features and design\. Iterate by communicating with it about UI adjustments and new features\.
*Command \(to TRAE Work\):* "I want to build a web page to manage all my viral content skills\. It should display titles, engagement metrics, and allow easy filtering\. Let’s design the UI and add features like a search bar\."

## Step 4: Mobile AI Office

To truly free yourself from your desk, leverage TRAE Work’s mobile capabilities:

- **Sync Across Devices**: Bind your PC, web, and mobile accounts for cloud synchronization\.

- **Delegate Tasks on the Go**: Assign tasks to AI from anywhere\. For example, dictate an idea into your phone, and TRAE Work will file it into your idea library or add it to your calendar\.

- **Remote Document Handling**: If you need to modify a document stored on your office computer, command TRAE Work via mobile to edit and send it\.
*Command \(via Mobile TRAE Work\):* "Edit \[document name\] as per \[client’s requirements\] and send it to \[email address\]\."

## The Human Touch in an AI\-Driven World

While AI excels at execution and structured thinking, it can’t replace **human emotional experiences and real\-life experiences**\. As you offload tasks to AI, you’ll find more time for:

- Team collaboration and communication\.

- Offline meetups and idea exchanges\.

- Personal life and relationships\.

In this new paradigm, AI handles the "work" and "labor", while you focus on "action"—the unique human elements of creativity, connection, and self\-expression\.

Start small, automate the repetitive, customize your skills, and build a workbench that grows with you\. The future of work is not about competing with AI, but about harnessing it to be more human\.
